Create a distinctive, high-quality personal portfolio for Pratik Bisen that presents him as an ambitious Information Technology engineering student and developing software engineer. The website should feel crafted, technically credible, visually memorable, and recruiter-friendly without pretending he has experience or mastery he has not confirmed.

- Education: B.Tech in Information Technology, Shri Guru Gobind Singhji Institute of Engineering and Technology (SGGSIE&T), Vishnupuri, Nanded, India.

## Career narrative
Position Pratik as a student building practical skills through software projects, data structures and algorithms, web development, Linux, and technical/community work. Long-term interests include software engineering and AI/ML. Use a confident, specific, evidence-led voice. Avoid exaggerated claims such as “expert,” “industry-leading,” or “master of everything.”

Confirm exact role titles, dates, contributions, and which details should be public before publishing. Do not assume a role is current if dates have changed.

## Design brief
- Chosen direction: immersive 3D glassmorphism.
- Themes: dark and light, both intentionally art-directed.
- Hero: identity-first rather than purely a conventional resume headline.
- Portrait: use a real portrait supplied by the owner; until supplied, create a polished abstract portrait frame or a clearly marked image placeholder. Do not synthesize a fake photo of the owner.
- The visual language should combine a dimensional ambient background, translucent glass surfaces, real backdrop blur, fine edge highlights, controlled reflections, layered depth, restrained 3D movement, and high text contrast.

## Product goals
1. Make a strong first impression in the first screenful.
2. Explain who Pratik is and what he is building.
3. Make selected projects easy to explore and verify.
4. Surface education, achievements, community work, and open-source contributions.
5. Make contact and profile links effortless.
6. Work well on mobile, keyboard navigation, reduced-motion settings, and lower-powered devices.
7. Remain easy for a student to maintain without editing complex component internals.

## Non-goals
- Do not turn the site into a generic template, a gaming dashboard, or an overloaded 3D demo.
- Do not add fake testimonials, fake company logos, fake client lists, fake GitHub statistics, fabricated achievements, or invented work experience.
- Do not require a backend or database unless a real feature requires it.
- Do not add a contact form that appears to submit but does not send. A mailto link is an acceptable initial contact path.
- Do not introduce a paid asset or API dependency without explicit approval.

## Missing inputs that must remain placeholders
- Real portrait image.
- Preferred public headline / short biography.
- Verified project URLs, descriptions, technology stacks, screenshots, and demo links.
- Resume PDF, if the owner wants to provide one.
- Confirmed dates and descriptions for leadership / achievements.
- Any public phone number or location details (do not add unless explicitly supplied for publication).

## Definition of success
The result looks intentionally designed at desktop and mobile sizes, the glass surfaces visibly interact with the background, both themes are polished, content is factually accurate, all navigation and links work, motion is purposeful and accessible, and the project can be installed, built, tested, and deployed using documented commands.
